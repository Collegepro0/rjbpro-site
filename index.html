Who We Are

Built by hand. Built to last.

Family-owned since 1992, RJB has grown from a Philadelphia-area coatings specialist into a national commercial contractor trusted by America's biggest retail brands.

Our Story

From one trade to one accountable partner.

Ron Bacskai founded RJB in 1992 just outside Philadelphia as a specialized provider of commercial coatings. The work was simple to describe and hard to do well: show up, do what you said, leave the store better than you found it.

That standard won repeat work, and repeat work demanded more capability. RJB grew trade by trade into a comprehensive commercial general contractor — self-performing paint, drywall, carpentry, and fixturing, and managing national vendor programs to the same specification we hold our own crews to.

Today RJB serves clients across 48 states from our 20,000-square-foot headquarters and training facility in Spring City, PA — where our teams define best practices before they ever reach your site.
